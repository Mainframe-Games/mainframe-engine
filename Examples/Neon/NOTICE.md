# Third-party assets in Neon

Neon's code, scenes and settings belong to Mainframe Engine and are MIT licensed (see the engine's `LICENSE`).
Everything under `Assets/` is generated by Neon's own code (`--write-scenes`), by its Blender scripts
(`Tools/apartment/build_apartment.py`: the apartment's geometry; `Tools/city/build_city_kit.py`: the city kit's
geometry, own work; `Tools/apartment/build_resident.py`: see "The resident"), by its poster script (`Tools/apartment/make_posters.py`), written by hand
(`Assets/Shaders/*.gdshader`, own work), copied from the Forest (`Assets/UI/prompt.rml`), or listed here. A file under
`Assets/` that is neither listed here nor generated is a release blocker.

| File | What | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Assets/Art/City/signs.png` | The neon signs' words (made-up names and plain words), rendered from the font by `build_city_kit.py`; the font itself is not shipped | Noto Sans JP Bold, © 2014–2021 Adobe (https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

All brands, signs and posters in Neon are original and fictional.

## Posters

`Assets/Art/Apartment/Textures/Poster_*.png` are original, drawn by `Tools/apartment/make_posters.py` with the engine's
fonts (Lato: SIL Open Font License 1.1; Roboto Mono: Apache 2.0). Their titles are fictional and their "glyph columns"
are random strokes, not text in any language.
