# Third-party assets in Neon

Neon's code, scenes and settings belong to Mainframe Engine and are MIT licensed (see the engine's `LICENSE`).
Everything under `Assets/` is generated by Neon's own code (`--write-scenes`), by its Blender script
(`Tools/apartment/build_apartment.py`: the apartment's geometry and flat colours, own work), or copied from the
Forest (`Assets/UI/prompt.rml`). There are no third-party assets yet; the textures, fonts and props of the later phases
will be listed here. A file under `Assets/` that is neither listed here nor generated is a release blocker.

All brands, signs and posters in Neon are original and fictional.
