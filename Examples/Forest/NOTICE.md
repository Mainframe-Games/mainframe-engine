# Third-party assets in the Forest

The Forest's code, scenes and settings belong to Mainframe Engine and are MIT licensed (see the engine's `LICENSE`).
Everything under `Assets/` is generated by the Forest's own code (`--write-scenes`), copied from the engine's game
template, or one of the third-party assets below. A file under `Assets/` that is neither listed here nor generated is a
release blocker.

The colour-grading LUT (`Assets/Grading/forest-morning.cube`) is generated as well (`ForestGrade`, its numbers fitted to
the reference shots by `Tools/grade_from_shots.py`, written by `--write-scenes`): own work, CC0.

The Forest's sounds are procedural (`Forest/Src/Audio`: seeded noise, filters and the engine's ZzFX port), synthesised
at run time: there are no audio files to list.

All third-party assets are **CC0 1.0** ([public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)):
no attribution is required, and it is given here as a courtesy. They come only from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) ("All ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
License", [docs.ambientcg.com/license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/)) and [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
("Our assets are all licensed as CC0", [polyhaven.com/license](https://polyhaven.com/license)); neither site's API has a
per-asset licence field, so the licence is the site-wide one. `Tools/fetch_assets.py` downloads them (plain HTTPS from
the official APIs and download hosts, Poly Haven files checked against their MD5) and converts them:

- **Terrain layers** (`Assets/Art/Terrain/<Layer>/`): ambientCG 2K JPG sets, resized to 1024² — colour and OpenGL
  normal (JPG), an ORM PNG packed from AmbientOcclusion (R), Roughness (G) and metallic 0 (B), and an 8-bit height PNG
  from Displacement.
- **Props** (`Assets/Art/Props/<id>/`): Poly Haven glTF 1K/2K; the grey roughness image is replaced by Poly Haven's ARM
  map of the same asset (occlusion, roughness, metallic) and wired as the glTF's occlusion and metallic-roughness
  texture; the fern's diffuse JPG and Alpha map are merged into one RGBA PNG.
- **Debris** (`Assets/Art/Debris/<id>/`, G8e.7): ambientCG 1K JPG sets of scattered leaves — colour and opacity merged
  into an RGBA PNG, the OpenGL normal (JPG) and an ORM PNG (occlusion 1, roughness, metallic 0).
- **Sky** (`Assets/Art/Sky/`): Poly Haven's tonemapped JPG of the HDRI, resized to 4096 × 2048.
- **Cabin textures** (`Assets/Art/Cabin/Textures/`): ambientCG 2K JPG sets and Poly Haven 2K maps (diffuse, OpenGL
  normal, ARM), resized to 1024² — colour and OpenGL normal (JPG) and an ORM PNG — for the log cabin's walls, log ends,
  floor, shingles, stone, door, wool, linen and leather.

The log cabin itself (`Assets/Art/Cabin/cabin.gltf` and `cabin.bin`) is original: modelled by `Tools/cabin/build_cabin.py`
in Blender (`just forest-cabin`), own work, CC0. Its furniture is the Poly Haven models below, placed at its sockets.
