# Third-party assets in the Forest

The Forest's code, scenes and settings belong to Mainframe Engine and are MIT licensed (see the engine's `LICENSE`).
Everything under `Content/` is generated by the Forest's own code (`--write-scenes`), copied from the engine's game
template, or one of the third-party assets below. A file under `Content/` that is neither listed here nor generated is a
release blocker.

The Forest's sounds are procedural (`Forest/Src/Audio`: seeded noise, filters and the engine's ZzFX port), synthesised
at run time: there are no audio files to list.

All third-party assets are **CC0 1.0** ([public domain dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)):
no attribution is required, and it is given here as a courtesy. They come only from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) ("All ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
License", [docs.ambientcg.com/license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/)) and [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)
("Our assets are all licensed as CC0", [polyhaven.com/license](https://polyhaven.com/license)); neither site's API has a
per-asset licence field, so the licence is the site-wide one. `Tools/fetch_assets.py` downloads them (plain HTTPS from
the official APIs and download hosts, Poly Haven files checked against their MD5) and converts them:

- **Terrain layers** (`Content/Art/Terrain/<Layer>/`): ambientCG 2K JPG sets, resized to 1024² — colour and OpenGL
  normal (JPG), an ORM PNG packed from AmbientOcclusion (R), Roughness (G) and metallic 0 (B), and an 8-bit height PNG
  from Displacement.
- **Props** (`Content/Art/Props/<id>/`): Poly Haven glTF 1K/2K; the grey roughness image is replaced by Poly Haven's ARM
  map of the same asset (occlusion, roughness, metallic) and wired as the glTF's occlusion and metallic-roughness
  texture; the fern's diffuse JPG and Alpha map are merged into one RGBA PNG.
- **Sky** (`Content/Art/Sky/`): Poly Haven's tonemapped JPG of the HDRI, resized to 4096 × 2048.
