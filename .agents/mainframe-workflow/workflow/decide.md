# Decide

Record a decision when the answer to "would a reader in six months ask why?" is yes.

1. Copy `.agents/mainframe-workflow/templates/decision.md` into the decisions folder.
2. Name it as `decision_naming` in `.agents/workflow.md` says. If numbered, take the next free number.
3. Keep to one decision per file.

## Changing your mind

New thinking supersedes; it does not edit. Write a new decision that links the old one. In the old one, change only the Status.
