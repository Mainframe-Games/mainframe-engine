# 2026-11-05: Video NLE, G20a to G20.2

Branch `feature/video-nle`. Plans: [G20a](../plans/2026-11-04-video-nle-g20a.md),
[G20b](../plans/2026-11-04-video-nle-g20b.md), [G20c](../plans/2026-11-04-video-nle-g20c.md),
[G20d](../plans/2026-11-05-video-nle-g20d.md), [G20.2](../plans/2026-11-05-video-nle-g20-2.md). Proposal:
[Video NLE](../../docs/design/future/video-nle.md); as built: [Video editing mode](../../docs/design/video-editor.md).

## What changed

- **Engine (G20a):** `SceneTree.FilesDropped`; `Assets/Video` editor-only (never built, published or exported); F10
  recordings into the project; `GpuTexture.Update2D` for in-place canvas textures; `Font.RenderLine` (G20.2).
- **Library (G20b, G20d, G20.2):** `MainframeEngine.Video/Timelines`: the `.mftimeline` model (version 2), edits,
  layout, snapping, reframing, the export and preview graphs, presets, `ClipDetection`, `CaptionPlanner`, transitions;
  `.mftranscript.json`; ranged silence detection; stills.
- **Editor (G20c, G20d, G20.2):** the old one-recording mode, `.mfcut` and Open in Cutback are gone; video mode is the
  timeline editor (media pool, Source and Timeline viewers through the export graph, Inspector, tracks, cut editor,
  export dialog), with Cutback's tools and the Transcript tab per clip, titles, images, transitions, fades, speed and
  captions.

## Decisions

- [ADR 0241](../decisions/0241-video-mode-nle.md) (with 0191 partly superseded). The builder's calls are in each plan's
  design notes and the doc's "Changes from the proposal": the preview plays through the export graph; transcripts per
  media file; detectors keep their own cuts outside a clip's window; titles rasterised on the CPU.

## Dead ends

- Allocation gate failures traced to closures over parameters and pattern variables (allocated at method entry even on
  early return), path strings rebuilt per frame, and `foreach` over `IReadOnlyList`: all fixed, each noted in a plan.
- A 0.5 s dissolve cannot be centred on whole frames at 30 fps; lengths are even frame counts.

## Verified

- `just build`, the Release build with `-warnaserror`, `just format-check`, `just shaders-check`, `check.sh`,
  `just test-video-ffmpeg` (7 real-ffmpeg tests: presets, preview, ranged silence, a dissolve/still/fade export),
  `just docs-build`.
- `just test`: editor, video and engine suites pass but the pre-existing `SurfaceShaderBuildTests`; the song-bake test
  is flaky under the full parallel run.
- `just test-render`: the video and Project Manager goldens (new `editor-video-title`) re-recorded on MoltenVK and
  lavapipe and inspected, allocation gates at 0 B; six goldens fail as they did before this work.
- `just test-linux` (x64 under Rosetta): editor and video pass; engine passes but
  `CrashProcessTests.WithTheDumpVariablesANativeFaultLeavesADump` (`createdump` under emulation, as in the ragdoll log).
  `just render-tests-linux`: 229 of 229 on lavapipe.
- `just qa-editor Tests/QA/video.qa` (16 captures, real ffmpeg and Whisper) and the README shot, inspected.

## Not verified

- A Finder drag by hand in a real game project (the drop is tested through `DropOsFiles`).
- Performance of three 1080p60 layers in the viewer on M1-class hardware.
