# 2026-11-15: G1c animation checks (G1.15)

Branch `feature/g1c-checks`, stacked on `feature/g1c-describe-edits` (#212). Plan:
[G1c checks](../plans/2026-11-15-g1c-checks.md). As built: [Animation and skinning → Checks](../../docs/design/animation.md#checks-g1c).

## What changed

- **Engine** (`MainframeEngine/Src/Animation/Authoring/AnimationChecks.cs`): `AnimationChecks.Run`,
  `AnimationCheckOptions` (+ `ForScript`), `AnimationCheckTolerances` (`With(name, value)`, `With("a=1, b=2")`),
  `AnimationCheckReport` (`HasErrors`, `Skipped`, `ToText`), `AnimationCheckFinding`, `CheckSeverity`.
- **Baker fix:** a pin holds through its release key (IK runs before the release is applied), so the legs no longer snap
  from the pinned crouch to the script's straight legs at the release key.
- **Tests:** `AnimationChecksTests` (15): a clean squat passes every check; the G1.13 test jump's problems are found
  (toes pinned pointing down at the landing, ankles past their range in the deep crouch, a fast arm swing, the end away
  from `stand`); a clip that breaks each check; tolerances; skipped checks; the pose restored. 108 tests in the folder.
- **Docs:** animation.md (Checks, Testing, the pin's release), the proposal (G1.15 built and its changes), AGENTS.md.

## Decisions

- None needing an ADR: the choices are the plan's design notes and the proposal's "Changes" entry for G1.15.

## Dead ends

- The first run on the test jump reported 26 errors. Most were real problems of the script (feet under the floor at
  the landing, ankles past their range, a fast arm swing, the hips dropping at full speed from straight knees: 32° of
  knee in the first frame), but the knee bending backwards (−167°) and toes 3.7 cm under the floor at 0.24 s came from
  the baker dropping the pins at the release key before solving IK. With that fixed, 8 errors remain, all the script's.
- The plan's "the baked test jump reports no errors" was wrong for that reason: the done-when became "a clean squat
  passes, and the test jump's real problems are reported". The test jump stays as it is (the baker tests use it).
