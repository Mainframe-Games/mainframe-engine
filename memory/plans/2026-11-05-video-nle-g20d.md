## Goal

Cutback's four tools come back, on the selected clip of a timeline:
- **Detect Silence**, **Detect Idle Gameplay**, **Transcribe** and **Remove Fillers** run on the selected clip (the video
  one of a linked pair), each result one undo step through `TimelineEdits.SetClipCuts`, so the link group cuts together
  and the clips after it ripple.
- **The transcript** is made once per media file and cached beside it as `<media>.mftranscript.json`, so every clip of
  that recording shares it.
- **The Inspector's Transcript tab** shows the selected clip's words inside its window, struck where cut, with the old
  panel's gestures.

## Not in scope

- G20.2 (fades, dissolves, titles, images, speed UI, auto-captions) and G20.3.
- Export Transcript (`.txt`/`.srt`): it needs the timeline's output times across clips; later, with captions (G20.2).

## Design notes (decided here)

- **Window-local results.** Each detector plans over the clip's `[In, Out]` only. Its cuts are then merged with that
  origin's existing cuts outside the window (`ClipDetection.InWindow`), and the partition replaces that origin as before
  (`ReplaceAutoSegments`, `ReplaceOriginCuts`). So a re-run in one window never drops the results of another, and manual
  cuts always survive (the partition's rule).
- **Silence detection reads only the window**: `SilenceDetector.DetectAsync` gains a range (`-ss`/`-t` before `-i`), and
  the spans come back in source time.
- **Transcripts are per media, whole file.** `TranscriptFile` (versioned JSON: source hash, model, words) is read when
  its hash matches the media's and written atomically after a transcription. `TranscriptStore` (editor) keeps the loaded
  ones by hash. The importer copies a transcript sidecar along like the capture sidecar; the pool already hides `.json`.
- **Fillers** use the cached transcript's words inside the window, located on the media's waveform from the media cache
  (Cutback's envelope and anchors), and fall back to the snapped word spans, as before. Without a transcript, Remove
  Fillers transcribes first and cuts when the words arrive.
- **Idle gameplay** reads `<media>.mfcap.json`. Without it the command is disabled, and its tooltip says why.
- **Jobs** live on the `TimelineTab` (one silence job and one transcription at a time), report "Cancel n %" on their
  toolbar buttons, and apply to the clip they started on if it still exists; the notice says what happened, in
  Cutback's sentences (the kept time a run removed inside the window).
- **Seams** for tests: `VideoView.SilenceDetector` and `VideoView.TranscriberFactory` (the old one), so no test runs
  ffmpeg or Whisper.

## Done when

- On a clip of a recording placed on a timeline: Detect Silence cuts only inside the clip's window, Transcribe fills the
  Transcript tab (and a second clip of the same recording shows it without transcribing), Remove Fillers cuts the fillers,
  Detect Idle Gameplay works with a sidecar and is disabled without one, a word click cuts the clip and its linked audio;
  each is one undo step and the clips after it ripple.
- The scenario tests above pass; the gates pass with the stated exceptions.
