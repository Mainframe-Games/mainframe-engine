# Plan: Video NLE G20.2, polish

**Status:** done (committed with G20.2)
**Docs:** [Video NLE proposal](../../docs/design/future/video-nle.md) (§ Phase 2 additions, scenarios G20.2),
[Video editing mode](../../docs/design/video-editor.md) (G20c/G20d as built), [ADR 0241](../decisions/0241-video-mode-nle.md),
[ADR 0118](../decisions/0118-managed-truetype-canvas-text.md) (the engine's TrueType rasteriser)
**Started:** 2026-11-05

## Goal

The proposal's phase 2, on the timeline editor as built:
- **Volume and fades:** clip volume (dB), fade in and out per clip (video from or to black, audio in volume), as
  Inspector fields and as handles on the clip's top corners.
- **Transitions** at an edit point: Cross Dissolve (video `xfade`, audio as overlapping fades) and Dip to Black, with a
  length; refused with a reason when the clips lack handle frames.
- **Titles:** title clips with text, font, size, colour, outline, shadow, box, alignment and position.
- **Images:** `.png`, `.jpg` and `.webp` as clips, 5 s by default, reframed like video.
- **Speed:** 0.25×–4× per clip from the Inspector (the model and graph have it since G20b).
- **Auto-captions:** Create Captions turns a clip's kept transcript words into title clips on a new track.

## Not in scope

- G20.3: nested timelines, proxies, roll/slip, sync lock, crash recovery for timelines.
- Keyframes of any kind, more transition types, title animation.
- A volume control in the track header (the Inspector's track gain stays the only one).

## Design notes (decided here)

- **File version 2.** `Clip` gains `Kind` (media, title, image), `VolumeDb`, `FadeIn`, `FadeOut` and `Title`; `Timeline`
  gains `Transitions`. Every new field has a default, and `TimelineMigrator` gets a 1 → 2 step that adds nothing, so
  version 1 files open unchanged.
- **Titles are rasterised on the CPU** by the engine's own TrueType rasteriser (a new public `Font.RenderLine`), not by
  a `Label2D` captured from a `SubViewport` as the proposal suggested: it needs no GPU (tests and headless runs make the
  same pixels), and the preview and export read the same PNG. `TitleRasterizer` (editor) composes the lines, outline,
  shadow and box at the timeline's size into `<project>/.mainframe/cache/video/titles/<hash>.png`, the hash taken from
  the style and size; a title clip's `MediaRef` carries that hash, so an edit makes a new PNG.
- **Still inputs** (titles, images) are `-loop 1 -framerate R -t len -i file`: no source seeking, and their media
  "duration" is an hour so any length fits.
- **Transitions** are per track, between two adjacent clips (`OutClip`, `InClip`), centred on the edit: each clip lends
  half the length from beyond its window (its handle). Adding one to a linked pair adds the matching one to the partners
  when they meet at the same edit. In the graph a dissolve replaces that edit's concat with `xfade` on the extended
  clips, and the audio fades overlap; Dip to Black is a fade out and a fade in of half the length each, with no handles.
- **Speed** changes go through one edit that ripples the clips after it (as cuts do), clamped to 0.25–4.
- **Captions** (`CaptionPlanner`, pure): the kept words of the clip's window, in timeline time, at most 7 words a
  caption, breaking at a pause of 0.5 s or more and after a sentence end; each caption a title clip in the caption style
  (bold, white with a dark outline, low in the frame) on a new video track above the clip's.

## Steps

- [x] **Model and file.** The new fields and records, version 2 with its migrator step, the serializer, validation of
  transitions. Verified by: serializer round-trip of every new field, a version 1 file opening as version 2.
- [x] **Edits.** `SetAudio` (volume, fades), `SetSpeed` (ripple), `SetTitle`, `PlaceStill` (titles and images, 5 s),
  `AddTransition`/`RemoveTransition` with `TransitionProblem` ("Not enough frames past the edit"), transitions dropped
  when their clips change. Verified by: `TimelineEditsTests.TransitionNeedsHandles` and the others.
- [x] **Graph.** Volume, fades (`fade … color=black`, `afade`), still inputs, dissolves (`xfade`) and dips, audio
  crossfades. Verified by: `TimelineGraphBuilderTests.CrossDissolve`, `SpeedChainsAtempo`, fades, volume, stills, and
  the opt-in ffmpeg test exporting a timeline with a dissolve, a title and an image.
- [x] **Titles.** `Font.RenderLine` in the engine (with an engine test); `TitleRasterizer` and its cache; the document
  resolves a title clip to its PNG. Verified by: `TitleRasterTests.PreviewAndExportShareRaster` and a raster check of
  outline, shadow and box.
- [x] **Editor.** Inspector sections (volume, fades, speed; the title's fields), fade handles on clip corners, transitions
  on the canvas (drawn over the edit, selected by click, length and kind in the Inspector, removed with Delete), Timeline
  menu: Add Title, Add Cross Dissolve (Cmd+D), Add Dip to Black, Create Captions; images importable and placeable.
  Verified by: workflow tests for each, the allocation tests.
- [x] **Captions.** `CaptionPlanner` and Create Captions. Verified by: `CaptionPlannerTests.BreaksAtPausesSkipsCuts` and a
  workflow test.
- [x] **Goldens, QA, docs.** `editor-video-title` render golden (a title over a clip with a dissolve) on both drivers;
  `video.qa` adds a title, a dissolve and captions; docs, proposal, milestones, README and the site.
  Verified by: every gate, the QA captures and goldens inspected by eye.

## Done when

- On a timeline: a clip's volume and fades change what plays and exports; a Cross Dissolve and a Dip to Black play at
  an edit (and a dissolve without handles is refused with its reason); a title shows over the picture in the viewer and
  the export alike; an image plays as a 5 s clip; a clip at 2× plays in half the time; Create Captions makes caption
  clips from a transcribed clip.
- The scenario tests above pass; the gates pass with the stated exceptions.
