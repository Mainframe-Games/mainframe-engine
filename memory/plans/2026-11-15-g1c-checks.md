## Goal

`AnimationChecks.Run` measures a clip on its humanoid rig (60 Hz plus every key time) and reports findings, each with a
check name, severity, time range, subject, the worst value and a suggested fix, for every check in the proposal's table:
ground, foot slide, range of motion, flips and spikes, loop, start and end, root, physics. Errors fail a check run;
warnings do not. Tolerances have defaults and can be set by name (`footSlide=0.03`), which `mfe anim check --tolerance`
will use.

## Design notes (decided here)

- Code in `MainframeEngine/Src/Animation/Authoring/`: `AnimationChecks`, `AnimationCheckOptions` (tolerances, the root
  mode, start and end poses, gravity), `AnimationCheckReport` + `AnimationCheckFinding` (text via `ToText`).
- Scripts are not kept, so what a script declared is passed in: `AnimationCheckOptions.ForScript(script, gravity)` sets
  the root mode and the first and last keys' named poses; without them those checks are skipped (`check` on a clip
  alone takes `--root` and `--start`/`--end` in G1.16).
- **Contacts** are measured, not read from pins (the clip does not keep them): a foot within 2 cm of the ground whose
  vertical speed is under 0.1 m/s. A contact's slide is its widest horizontal distance from where it began.
- Consecutive failing samples of one check and subject become one finding with its time range and worst value.
- **Ground** uses bone origins (feet, toes, hands, hips) against the rest pose's lowest foot or toe, as `describe` does.
- **Flips** are adjacent keys of a rotation track in opposite hemispheres; **spikes** are sampled angular speeds.
- **Loop:** `Linear` compares the pose and the per-frame motion at both ends; `PingPong` wants both ends at rest.
- **Start and end** compare joint angles only (not the hips offset) with the named pose.
- **Root:** `InPlace` warns on hips XZ drift; `Motion` errors on a hips jump faster than 30 m/s and warns on yaw faster
  than 180°/s. **Physics** (`Motion` only): each flight phase (both feet off the ground) whose hips apex is more than
  25 % off `g·T²/8`.
