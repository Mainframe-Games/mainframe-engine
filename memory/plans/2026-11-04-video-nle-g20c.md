# Plan: Video NLE G20c, the editor

**Status:** done (committed with G20c)
**Docs:** [Video NLE proposal](../../docs/design/future/video-nle.md) (§ What the owner sees, § Preview, § Export,
scenarios G20c), [ADR 0241](../decisions/0241-video-mode-nle.md), [Video editing mode](../../docs/design/video-editor.md),
[Editor](../../docs/design/editor.md#editor-modes), G20b's library (`MainframeEngine.Video/Timelines/`)
**Started:** 2026-11-04

## Goal

Video mode is the timeline editor, inside the open game project:
- **Layout:** media pool, Source viewer, Timeline viewer and Inspector on top; the multi-track timeline with track
  headers below it; the cut editor at the bottom.
- **Importing:** from a picker or a Finder drop, copied into `Assets/Video/Media`.
- **Editing:** every phase-1 edit with Premiere's keys, reframing, and export through the presets.
- **Removed:** the old one-recording mode, `.mfcut`, Recent Videos and Open in Cutback.

## Not in scope

- **The per-clip Cutback tools** (Transcribe, Remove Fillers, Detect Silence, Detect Idle Gameplay) and the Transcript
  tab: G20d. In G20c the cut editor edits cuts by hand only. Its menu items and toolbar buttons are gone until G20d.
- **Phase 2:** titles, images, transitions, volume and fades, speed UI, captions.
- **Nested timelines:** phase 3.

## Design notes (decided here)

- **The preview plays through the export graph** (a change from the proposal's sprite compositor):
  - The Timeline viewer runs `TimelineGraphBuilder` at preview size: the long side ≤ 960 px, ≤ 30 fps, raw RGBA, one
    video graph and one audio graph per play.
  - It runs through today's `VideoPreview` machinery: gapless decoders, the audio voice as the clock, newest-wins
    scrubbing.
  - `VideoPreview` gets an `IPreviewProgram` (the source of decode arguments): `SourceProgram` (one file, the Source
    viewer and the cut editor) and `TimelineProgram`.
  - What the viewer shows is exactly what export writes, titles and dissolves included later.
  - The cost: an edit restarts the decode, as an edit already does today.
- **Undo:** `TimelineDocument` commits `TimelineEdit(before, after)` snapshots to the editor's `UndoRedo`, with a merge
  key per drag or field. "Nothing changed" is reference equality with the library's result.
- **Cut editor:** today's `VideoTimelineView`, `VideoTimelineController` and `VideoTimelineCanvas`, over a `CutTarget`
  adapter. The adapter shows the selected clip's segments and maps their edits to
  `TimelineEdits.SetClipCuts`. It is limited to the clip's window.
- **Media pool:** an RmlUi panel over `ProjectFileSystem` rooted at `Assets/Video`, filtered by `FileKinds` to video,
  audio, image and timeline. Posters are made by ffmpeg into `.mainframe/cache/video/`.
- **Mode needs a project:** `EditorMode.UsesProject()` is true for Video, so project switches close video tabs like
  scene tabs.

## Done when

- In a game project, the owner's flow works by hand:
  1. drag clips from Finder;
  2. create a 1080×1920 timeline;
  3. razor, ripple delete and unlink;
  4. reframe;
  5. add music on A2;
  6. export the vertical preset.

  ffprobe reports 1080×1920 at 30 fps with the expected duration. (QA scripts all of this except the Finder drag, which
  is a hand check.)
- The old mode's code and `.mfcut` support are gone, and no test references them.
- The gates above pass, with the stated exceptions.

## Changes while building

- **Tests:** the scenario tests live in `VideoWorkflowTests` (Finder drops on the timeline and the pool, Add Edit, Open in
  Video Editor, the export dialog and job, the Inspector, the track menu, the program's graph per visible track) and
  `EditorModeTests.VideoModeNeedsAProject`; `TimelineModelTests.cs` holds the document, pool, importer and gesture tests,
  `TimelineAllocationTests` the headless 0 B check. `CutEditorTests.RestoreRipplesLater` stayed a hand check (the cut
  editor's gestures keep their `VideoTimelineControllerTests`).
- **The pool follows a project only in video mode** (or when a video command asks), and makes
  `Assets/Video/{Recordings,Media,Timelines}` then: before, opening any project in game mode created `Assets/Video`.
- **Found by the allocation gate:** closures over parameters and pattern variables allocated on every call even when
  they returned early (`PollJobs`, the media cache's lookups), `TimelineDocument.Resolve` built a path per clip per
  frame (now cached), and `foreach` over `IReadOnlyList` lanes boxed its enumerator.
- **Layout:** the timeline is 240 dp by default and its lanes 44 dp (video) and 34 dp (audio), so V1–V3 and A1–A2 fit
  a 720 dp window; the track headers' right-click menu adds and removes tracks.
- **Trim at a cut** takes the edge on the pointer's side.
- **Inspector fields** commit one undo step per commit (Enter or blur), not merged per field.
- The proposal's other changes (the preview through the export graph, J/K/L, the one-file picker) are in
  [Video editing mode](../../docs/design/video-editor.md#changes-from-the-proposal).
